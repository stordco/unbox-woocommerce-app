Integrate with Stord Unbox to share data to power personalised printing.

== Description ==

Stord Unbox (formerly Penny Black) lets you create personalised prints for your customers, elevate the unboxing moment, and take advantage of a high-open-rate physical channel.

Create visual campaigns, segment your customers, and use personalised inserts for engagement, retention, and upsell. Then use the Unbox analytics platform to track QR code scans and see your ROI.

This WooCommerce plugin connects your store to Unbox and transmits order information when orders are placed. Unbox uses that data to segment customers and generate the right PDF for each order.

If you fulfil your own orders, you can also trigger prints from the WooCommerce orders admin. Unbox inserts are usually printed at pack; self-fulfilment merchants can request those prints directly from WooCommerce.

The plugin requires an Unbox account to send orders and trigger prints. Find out more at [https://www.stord.com/unbox](https://www.stord.com/unbox).

Once installed, use the Stord Unbox tab on the WooCommerce settings page to configure the plugin.
